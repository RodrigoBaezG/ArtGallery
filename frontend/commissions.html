Custom watercolor

Commissions

Watercolor illustrations made for your project, from first idea to finished piece.

What I take on

  • Editorial illustration

    Illustrations for magazines, newspapers and online publications.

  • Book illustration

    Covers and interior illustrations for books.

  • Personal commissions

    A watercolor painted from your references: a portrait, a place, an object or a moment you want to keep.

  • Original artwork

    Interested in an original from the gallery? Ask me about its availability.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your project

    Send a short message with what you have in mind, your references and your deadline.

  2. Quote and timeline

    I reply with a quote and an estimated timeline. Work starts once we've agreed on both.

  3. Sketch

    I send you a sketch to review before I start painting.

  4. Painting

    Once the sketch is approved, I paint the final watercolor.

  5. Delivery

    I deliver the finished illustration in the format agreed in the quote.

Timelines

Timelines depend on size and complexity. I'll give you an estimate with every quote.

Working to a fixed date? Mention it in your first message.

Every project is quoted individually, based on size, complexity and how the illustration will be used.

What to send

To give you an accurate quote, include:

References
Photos or any images that capture the idea.
Size
The final dimensions (e.g. A4 or 30 × 40 cm), or the print or screen format.
Use
Where the illustration will appear: a book, a magazine, a website, a gift…
Deadline
The date you need it by.
Budget
If you have one in mind, it helps me suggest the best option.
Extra notes
Palette, mood, style or any other detail that helps.

Ready to start?

Tell me what you have in mind and I'll get back to you with the next steps.
